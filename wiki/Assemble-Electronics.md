# Step 3: Assemble the electronics

The electronics are four boards stacked together: the custom Tumbly PCB, the Feather M0 Adalogger, the DS3231 RTC FeatherWing, and the OLED FeatherWing.

## 3.1 Order the custom PCB
Order it from OSH Park:

<a href="https://oshpark.com/shared_projects/LoxoiB0y"><img src="https://oshpark.com/assets/badge-5b7ec47045b78aef6eb9d83b3bac6b1920de805e9a0c227658eac6e19a045b9c.png" alt="Order from OSH Park"></a>

Or download the `.brd` and `.sch` files from [extras/PCB](https://github.com/KravitzLabDevices/Tumbly/tree/main/extras/PCB) and have it made anywhere.

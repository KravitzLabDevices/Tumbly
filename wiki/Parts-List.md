# Step 1: Gather parts

The full parts list, with part numbers and suppliers, is kept in a Google Sheet so it stays up to date:

### 👉 [Tumbly parts list](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1O0kQEswAetQ5PXhv6P8ZYKxMUGYi77xnT9NAyXGhbck/edit?usp=sharing)

You will also need:
- **The custom PCB.** You can order it from OSH Park in [Step 3](Assemble-Electronics).
- **Access to a 3D printer.** See [Step 2](3D-prints).
- **A microSD card.** The card you choose affects battery life; see [Power consumption](Power-Consumption).
- **A soldering iron**, solder, and a micro-USB data cable.

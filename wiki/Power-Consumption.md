# Power consumption

While asleep, Tumbly draws about **1–2 mA**. The exact amount [depends on the SD card](https://neurotechhub.wustl.edu/micro-sd-card-low-power-showdown-part-ii/). With a 2200 mAh battery, that works out to roughly **50–100 days** per charge.
